**Brawl.** To fight in a heated melee.
**Command.** To get your way through orders or threats.
**Deceive.** To lie, hide, or pretend.  
**Discern.** To perceive the realities of a situation.
**Endure.** To stand your ground and exert yourself.
**Hunt.** To track, pursue, and shoot at range. 
**Mend.** To fix items and heal people.  
**Parley.** To win hearts and minds.  
**Prowl.** To sneak, climb, and jump.
**Rush.** To run, react, or act with haste.
