
### Gather Dice
* You start with a d6 for luck.

There are various ways to gain dice. Your final dice pool may never exceed 5. 
* Add a d6 for a relevant **Skill**
* Add a d6 for a relevant **Domain**
* Add a d6 for a relevant **Mastery**

You may also gain 1-2d6 for various **Advantages**. These include:
* Exploiting the situation or environment
* Having a particularly relevant **Item**
* Having an ally providing **Aid**

### Difficulty
If an action is particularly difficult, the DM may remove dice. 

Some reasons why an action may be difficult:
* The approach is particularly tricky or convoluted 
* The challenge or foe is resistant against this approach
* The challenge or foe is much larger than you in scale or number
* Your character suffers from **Harm** that is hindering their efforts

| Difficulty | Penalty | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Difficult  |  1 | Fighting with a trained militiaman |
| Arduous  |  2 | Sparring with a master swordsman |
| Extreme  |  3 | Charging a dragon head-on |

### The Outcome
Roll your dice. The outcome is determined by your highest die rolled:

**6: A Strong hit.** You get what you want. 
**4-5: A Weak hit.** You get what you want, but at a price. Take stress.
**1-3: Failure.** You do not get what you want. Take stress.
