### Drone
When you use *Sporeburst*, spend another **Blood** and roll **Command** + **Wild** to try to take control of one creature within range. On a success, you gain control over them until the end of the situation. They will not do anything obviously harmful to themself, and will be released if they take harm. 
- **Potent Spores:** You can use *Drone* on a small group at a time.
- **Infestation:** At your will, you may relinquish control of your target to initiate another Sporeburst at their location. 
- **Reinhabit:** If you use *Drone* on a corpse, you may change bodies with your own, leaving it to rot. Doing so clears your **Blood** stress, but you take 2 stress to **Resolve**.
