You were the heir to an ancient dynasty. Now, through political machinations or a terrible trespass, you find yourself disinherited from your birthright. How do you use the remnants of your authority and connections?

Skill: **Command**
Domain: **Order**

### Bonds
Ask each of your party members one of the following, and mark a bond:
- *My family took you under our wing- what did you do to help us?*
- *What do you think about my noble upbringing?*

# Core Abilities

### Influence
You collect **influence** dice. At the start of each *Chapter*, gain 3 influence. You can use influence to roll with **Mastery** when asserting your authority or highborn status.

### Loyal Retainer
You have one last loyal follower. They are an **Asset** which will not leave your side. They will not die- at least without being replaced- but they may get into trouble.

When your retainer performs an action, you can spend **Influence** to give them **Mastery**.

### Letters from Afar
*Camp Action*
You open a letter sealed in wax. Gain a **Secret**.

# Advancements

### Negotiator
Gain the **Parley** skill. On a successful **Parley** roll, gain **Influence**.

### Duelist
Gain the **Brawl** skill. When you engage in one-on-one combat, you roll with mastery.

### Court Gossip
Gain the **Deceive** skill. Once per session, spend a secret to reveal a great scandal 

### Secret Society
Gain the **Arcane** domain. You know the covert signs and symbols of an esoteric society embedded across the realm. Once per chapter, while amongst civilization, you may use this to get help from a local initiate. 

### Ancestral 
Gain the **Lore** domain.

### Battlefield Training
Gain the **War** domain. You can use your influence to give your party members **Mastery** in combat.
