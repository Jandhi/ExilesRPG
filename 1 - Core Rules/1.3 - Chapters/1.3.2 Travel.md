Journey Chapters follow the party as they travel between locations. The world is vast, and the road between places is rarely safe or uneventful.

# The Road

During a Journey, the Guide presents **Road Scenes** -- moments along the way that bring the world to life. A Journey will typically have 2-3 Road Scenes before the party reaches their destination.

Each Road Scene is tied to a **Domain** that reflects the terrain or the nature of what the party encounters.

## Vistas
A moment of atmosphere. The party crests a hill and sees the valley below, choked with pale fungus. A ruined aqueduct stretches across the gorge. The treeline ends and the moor begins, flat and featureless to the horizon.

Vistas don't require rolls. They set the tone, establish the Domain of the area, and give the party information about what lies ahead or around them.

## Encounters
Something on the road demands a response. A washed-out ford. A stranger who won't move aside. A storm rolling in. A beast stalking the party from the treeline.

Encounters typically require one or two action rolls to resolve. They are not full adventures -- they are moments of tension that the party must navigate before moving on.

## Revelations
Information that changes the picture. A body wearing the sigil of the faction you're heading toward. Smoke rising from where the village should be. A trail of something that shouldn't exist this far from the Blight.

Revelations recontextualise the destination or the journey itself. They might cause the party to change course, prepare differently, or arrive with new questions.

# Travel Actions

At the start of a Journey, each player chooses a **Travel Action** -- something their character does while on the move. One player **must** choose to **Navigate**; the rest choose freely from the common list below or from a *Travel Action* on their playbook.

Travel Actions resolve during the Road, woven into the fiction between scenes.

## Navigate (Mandatory)
Keep the party on course. Make an action roll, typically using **Discern** and a relevant Domain.

On a **Strong Hit**, the party arrives where they intended without complication.

On a **Weak Hit**, the party arrives, but the journey costs them -- extra time, stress, or unwanted attention.

On a **Failure**, the party gets lost. The Guide introduces an unplanned Road Scene, or the party arrives somewhere they didn't intend.

## Scout
Range ahead and look for danger or opportunity. Make an action roll, typically using **Hunt** or **Discern**.

On a hit, the Guide reveals information about an upcoming Road Scene before the party walks into it -- the nature of a threat, a shortcut, or a place of interest off the path.

## Forage
Gather food, water, materials, or useful items along the way.

Refresh 1 **Favour**.

## Keep Watch
Stay alert and guard the group against threats.

Refresh 1 **Fate**.

## Playbook Travel Actions
Some playbook abilities are marked with *Travel Action*. These replace a common Travel Action -- a character who uses their playbook's Travel Action does not also pick from the common list.
