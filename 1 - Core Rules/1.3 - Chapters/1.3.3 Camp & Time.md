At the end of a Chapter, the players may **Make Camp**. This is one of the primary ways of reducing stress and harm.
## Camp Actions
When in camp, the players can take 2 camp actions. 
### Rest
Refresh 2 **Blood**
### Reminisce
Refresh 2 **Resolve**
### Take Watch
Refresh 2 **Fate**
### Forage
Refresh 2 **Favour**
### Heal
Remove **Minor Harm**, or reduce **Major** harm to **Minor**
## Reflection
After taking their camp actions, the players recount what they did during the session to tally XP.

For each of the following, the players gain 1 XP if they did it once or a little bit, and 2 if they did it multiple times.

- You expressed your beliefs, drives, heritage, or background
- You explored your **Bonds** with other characters
- You made desperate rolls or took Major fallout
### Advancement
After Reflection, players can spend 4 XP to gain a minor ability or ability upgrade, or 8 XP for a major ability.
## Time Passes
Each relevant faction in the Arc makes a roll to advance their objects. This will be affected by the players' actions. 

The faction rolls their relevant skills and domains.
