The game is separated into *Chapters* and *Arcs*. 

A Chapter involves the players pursuing a singular objective, which may have a self-contained plot line or chain together with previous chapters. The players will always gain XP at the end of a chapter. A session will usually consist of one or two Chapters.

An Arc revolves about the players pursuing a long term objective. It will often focus on a certain location or villain. 

# Chapters

### Beginning a Chapter
At the start of a chapter, the party will determine the group's objective and the type of chapter.
#### Travel Chapters
Travel chapters are somewhat different to others, as they represent a much longer passage of time as the characters venture great distances. They will consist of a number of scenes and vistas as the characters happen upon different locations on their way to their destination. This is further described in [[1.3.2 Travel]].

Some abilities are only available during travel chapters. These are marked with *Travel Action*.
### Ending a Chapter
At the end of each chapter, the players make camp, tally XP, and time passes. This is described in detail in [[1.3.3 Camp & Time]].
