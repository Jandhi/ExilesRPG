# Minor Fallout
### Blood
 - **Worn.** You are exhausted. You cannot gain dice from **Skills**.
 - **Limping.** Your mobility is impaired. Any tasks involving quick movement will be **difficult**.
 - **Disarmed.** You lose your grip and drop something important.
### Resolve
 - **Shaken.** You can't seem to think straight. You cannot gain dice from **Domains.**
- **Panic.** You rely on your base instincts: *fight, flight, or freeze*.
- **Overindulge.** You have too much to drink, get caught up in a card game, or are wooed away by a pretty face 

### Favour
- **Insulted.** You have made an enemy of someone influential in the local haven. They're going to make your life difficult. 
- **Shattered.** An unimportant item is destroyed, or an important item gains \[*broken*] 
- **Lawbreaker.** You have broken some minor local law or custom, and face a penalty. 

### Fate
- **Lost.** You lose your way, either together or separated from the group.
- **Shattered.** An unimportant item is destroyed, or an important item gains \[*broken*] 

### Corruption
- **Unnerving.** Your appearance betrays your warped nature. Social encounters become **difficult.**
- **Whispers.** You hear voices when alone or in the dark. The GM may feed you false or half-true information at any time.
- **Visions.** You receive sporadic sights of the past and future. When you do, the GM will provide you with some information, and you take 1 stress to **Resolve.**
