## Describe
The first step to any action roll is for the player to describe what their character is doing in the fiction. 
## Position
The Guide will describe your **Position** — this tells you the stakes of the situation and how much stress you take on a weak hit or failure.

| Position       | Description                                            | Stress |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ | ------ |
| **Controlled** | You have the upper hand. Do you keep your momentum?    | 1      |
| **Risky**      | Fate is on a knife's edge. Things could go either way. | 2      |
| **Desperate**  | You're in trouble. Any mistake will cost you dearly.   | 3+     |
## Gather Dice
Your dice pool starts with a d6 for luck. There are various ways to gain dice.
* Add a d6 for a relevant **Skill**
* Add a d6 for a relevant **Domain**
* Add a d6 for a relevant **Mastery**

You may also gain various **Edges**. Each Edge gives an extra d6. These include:
* Exploiting the situation or environment
* Having a particularly relevant **Item**
* Having an ally providing **Aid**

## Difficulty
If an action is particularly difficult, the Guide may remove the X highest dice.

Some reasons why an action may be difficult:
* The approach is particularly tricky or convoluted 
* The challenge or foe is resistant against this approach
* The challenge or foe is much larger than you in scale or number
* Your character suffers from **Harm** that is hindering their efforts

| Difficulty | Penalty | Example                            |
| ---------- | ------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Difficult  | 1       | Fighting with a trained militiaman |
| Arduous    | 2       | Sparring with a master swordsman   |
| Impossible | 3       | Charging a dragon head-on          |
## The Outcome
The highest die determines the outcome

**6: A Strong hit.** You get what you want.
**4-5: A Weak hit.** You get what you want, but take stress based on your Position.
**1-3: Failure.** You do not get what you want, and take stress based on your Position.

### Criticals
If you roll multiple 6s, it is a critical hit. You gain additional benefits. 
If your highest die is a 1, it is a critical failure, and you suffer additional penalties.
