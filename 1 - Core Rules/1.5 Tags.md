## Item Tags
**Broken** - This item is unusable until mended
**Close** - This weapon is ineffective outside of close range
**Concealed** - This weapon is not obvious on your person
**Deadly** - This weapon is especially lethal. Your hits have more effect. 
**Huge** - This item is difficult to use in close quarters
**Reach** - This weapon is effective at an extended range
**Piercing** - This weapon ignores armour

## Character Tags
**Armoured** - Attacks are less effective against this character
