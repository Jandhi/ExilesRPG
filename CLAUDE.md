# Exiles RPG — Context for Claude

This is a TTRPG project written in Obsidian (markdown with `[[wikilinks]]`). When writing or editing rules content, match the existing voice: second-person ("you"), em-dashes, bold for key terms, concise and evocative.

## The Fantasy

Dark fantasy, **dark but hopeful**. The world is dangerous and full of mystery, but there is light if you fight for it. Think the fellowship and journey of *Lord of the Rings* with the cryptic, layered worldbuilding of *Dark Souls* — but brighter, warmer, more human.

Players should feel **curiosity and adventure** tempered by real struggle. This is a game about a fellowship on the road ahead, discovering what the world holds and who they're becoming.

## The Table

- **3–5 players** plus a Guide (the GM term in this system)
- Works at **any campaign length** — short arcs to long campaigns
- Guide prep target is **light** (a page of notes), with **near-zero** being possible — the system provides seeds, tables, and fiction-first tools so that cool stories emerge without heavy prep

## What This Game Is Not

- Not a **tactical combat sim** — no grid, no feat trees, no build optimization
- Not a **doom spiral** — the world is hard but not hopeless, and player agency is never undermined
- Not rules for every situation — the fiction and the Guide fill gaps, not a rulebook

## Design Principles

1. **Simple to play.** Players should be able to pick it up fast. No situation where play halts because someone doesn't know what to do.
2. **Simple to run.** Little to no balancing required. Pre-generated content and generative systems provide seeds to improvise from.
3. **Inspiring.** Thematic content that makes people want to create characters and tell stories. The Domains, playbooks, and fallout tables should spark imagination.
4. **Fiction-first.** Rules encourage roleplay, exploration of character drives, and improvisation. Abilities encourage players to spend rather than hoard, act rather than plan, and embrace interesting hardship.
5. **Collaborative worldbuilding.** The world belongs to the whole table, not just the Guide. Players introduce facts and details — the Guide shapes what they mean.
6. **Modular.** Subsystems can be inserted or removed to fit the campaign and setting.
7. **A solid gameplay loop.** Horizon → Chapter → Camp creates variable pacing with natural moments of rest between action.

## Key System Details

- **10 Domains, 10 Skills** — Domains are the lens for the entire world (not just magic)
- **d6 dice pool** with stress/resistance/fallout system
- **Session structure:** Horizon → Chapter → Camp
- **Three chapter types:** Journey, Venture, Crisis
- **Trinkets & Secrets** — narrative resources tied to Domains, capped at 3 each
- **Fallout spiral** — consequences that transform characters, not just hurt them
